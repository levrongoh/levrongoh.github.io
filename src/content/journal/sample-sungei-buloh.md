I got there just after the gates opened. The tide was coming in fast, pushing the waders up onto the last strips of mud. Everything smelled of salt and mangrove.

## The crocodile

About forty minutes in, a long, dark shape slid onto the bank below the boardwalk. It stayed there for nearly an hour, perfectly still.

## Field notes

- **Tide:** 2.9 m high at 07:40
- **Highlights:** Estuarine Crocodile, Collared Kingfisher, Whimbrel
- **Tip:** The main bridge gives the best low angle onto the mudflats
