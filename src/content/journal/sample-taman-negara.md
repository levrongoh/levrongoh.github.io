Seven days in a forest that's said to be around 130 million years old.

## Day 1: Kuala Tahan

We took the river up to Kuala Tahan. Hornbills flew over twice before we'd even unpacked.

## Day 3: The canopy walkway

At first light the canopy filled with mist, and a pair of Black-and-yellow Broadbills worked the vines right at eye level.

## Gear that earned its place

- 150–600mm for the canopy
- A rain cover (it rained every afternoon)
- Leech socks. Not optional.
